# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Research Swarm for Claude Code is a welcoming, safe, and equitable collaboration space. Participants and maintainers are expected to engage in good faith and to respect the dignity, safety, privacy, and contributions of others, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, honest, and constructive; give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints, stated boundaries, confidentiality, and privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm where practical.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately, especially when discussing research evidence.
- Keep collaboration focused on the repository and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

This includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal or character attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted personal attention after a request to stop, impersonation, doxxing, disclosing private information without permission, and conduct that endangers others or the community. It also includes knowingly misrepresenting authorship or sources, evading enforcement, or using project spaces for off-topic commercial promotion.

## Scope

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, documentation, and any project-managed communication or event. It also applies when someone represents this project in a public space.

## Reporting and privacy

Report a concern promptly. Do not post incident details publicly. This repository does not currently publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting address. As a temporary contact path, open a GitHub issue requesting a private conduct-reporting channel and include **no incident details**; maintainers should provide a private channel before requesting them. For abuse involving GitHub itself or an immediate safety concern, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools and local emergency services as appropriate.

Project maintainers review reports fairly and as promptly as their capacity allows. They share information only with people needed to investigate or respond, except where disclosure is necessary for safety or law. A maintainer who is involved in a report must recuse from handling it. This process does not promise a particular outcome or absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may investigate reported conduct, seek relevant context, and take proportionate action. Depending on the impact and seriousness, actions may include a private warning and repair request, a time-limited restriction on participation, temporary suspension, or removal from project spaces. Serious conduct can skip lower steps. When appropriate, maintainers communicate the reason and conditions for an action while protecting safety and confidentiality.
