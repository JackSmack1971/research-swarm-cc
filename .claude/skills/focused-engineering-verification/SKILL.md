# T3 focused adversarial verification

Use a distinct fresh context. Receive only the scoped Knowledge Need, admitted
T2 claims, source metadata and locators, relevant repository facts, and the
disconfirming-first posture. Do not receive researcher rationale or unrelated
history.

Return one terminal disposition for every admitted claim, checked source IDs,
specific rationale, conflicts, uncertainty, and T4 escalation where required.
Use `unverifiable` for unavailable or insufficient evidence, never
`contradicted`. Do not write files, produce a report, synthesize broad
conclusions, authorize a decision, or spawn agents. Broad scope, unresolved
material conflict, substantial source diversity, and high-consequence needs
remain T4 concerns.
