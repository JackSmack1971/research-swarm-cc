# T2 focused engineering research

Use a fresh context and receive only the scoped Knowledge Need, relevant T0/T1
facts, and explicit source, claim, and web-call limits. Research only the
remaining question and reuse evidence that already answers a subquestion.

Use the canonical research standards: prefer official documentation,
standards, vendor advisories, primary research, and other authoritative
sources; record provenance, precise locators, dates, version applicability,
independence, confidence rationale, counter-evidence, gaps, and budget use.
Return only the validated focused-research evidence object. Do not write files,
produce a report, broaden scope, spawn agents, or make a decision. Missing
authority, material conflict, low confidence, an unanswered stop condition, or
budget overflow escalates to T3 or T4.
